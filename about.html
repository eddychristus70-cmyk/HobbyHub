About HobbyHub

A student capstone project connecting local hobbyists.

The Problem

Students and young adults often pick up hobbies — then outgrow them, run out of space, or want to try something new — while paying full price for items that could just as easily be swapped locally. HobbyHub makes it easy to find someone nearby who has what you want, or wants what you have.

Who It's For

HobbyHub is built for students and young adults in a local community — anyone with a hobby, a bit of gear, or a skill worth sharing, and a tight budget that makes swapping more appealing than buying new.
